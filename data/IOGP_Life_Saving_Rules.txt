These are the 9 official IOGP Life-Saving Rules used in the oil & gas industry.
Each rule includes:
- Rule Name & ID
- Description (When does this rule apply?)
- Keywords (Words that appear in reports related to this rule)
- Example Phrases (Realistic report snippets)

================================================================================

RULE 1: ENERGY ISOLATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Description:
Failure to isolate hazardous energy sources (electrical, mechanical, chemical, 
thermal, or pressure) before maintenance, repair, or cleaning activities.

Keywords:
lockout, tagout, isolation, energy source, de-energize, electrical panel, 
breaker, valve closed, steam, pressure, pneumatic, hydraulic, stored energy

Example Phrases:
- "failed to lock out"
- "did not isolate the electrical panel"
- "left the valve open"
- "didn't de-energize before maintenance"
- "no tagout on the breaker"
- "bypassed the safety lock"

================================================================================

RULE 2: HOT WORK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Description:
Any work involving open flames, sparks, heat sources, or welding/cutting/grinding 
activities near flammable materials, gas lines, or explosive atmospheres.

Keywords:
welding, grinding, cutting, soldering, spark, flame, ignition source, gas leak, 
flammable, explosive, hot work permit, torch, brazing, arc welding

Example Phrases:
- "welding near a gas pipe"
- "grinding sparks hit flammable material"
- "cutting pipe without fire watch"
- "open flame near gas leak"
- "hot work without a permit"
- "ignition source present in hazardous area"

================================================================================

RULE 3: CONFINED SPACE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Description:
Entering or working in a confined space (tank, vessel, silo, manhole, sewer, pit)
without proper gas testing, ventilation, atmospheric monitoring, or entry permit.

Keywords:
confined space, tank, vessel, silo, manhole, sewer, pit, gas test, oxygen level, 
ventilation, atmosphere, entry permit, toxic gas, nitrogen, hydrogen sulfide

Example Phrases:
- "entered a tank without gas testing"
- "no oxygen check before entry"
- "did not test the atmosphere"
- "confined space without a permit"
- "no ventilation in the vessel"
- "H2S detected during entry"

================================================================================

RULE 4: LINE OF FIRE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Description:
Being in the path of moving equipment, vehicles, suspended loads, pressure 
releases, or any hazard where the worker is exposed to a potential strike or 
impact.

Keywords:
line of fire, moving equipment, crane load, pressure release, vehicle path, 
trajectory, caught between, swing radius, blind spot, heavy load, dropped object

Example Phrases:
- "standing under a suspended load"
- "in the path of moving equipment"
- "crane lifting above workers"
- "caught between two objects"
- "no exclusion zone around the crane"
- "moved into the danger zone"

================================================================================

RULE 5: LIFTING OPERATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Description:
Unsafe lifting practices including overloading cranes/hoists, unstable loads, 
improper rigging, defective slings, or lack of hand signals and tag lines.

Keywords:
lifting, crane, hoist, rigging, sling, chain, shackle, spreader bar, overload, 
unstable load, hand signals, load capacity, tag line

Example Phrases:
- "sling broke during lift"
- "overloaded the crane"
- "improper rigging caused load swing"
- "lifted without a tag line"
- "load swung dangerously"
- "exceeded the load capacity"

================================================================================

RULE 6: PERMIT TO WORK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Description:
Work carried out without a valid work permit, outside the scope of the permit, 
or after the permit has expired. Includes non-routine maintenance and high-risk 
activities.

Keywords:
permit, authorization, work order, approval, written permit, without permit, 
expired permit, scope change, non-routine, authority to work

Example Phrases:
- "started work without a permit"
- "permit had expired"
- "worked outside the permit scope"
- "no written authorization"
- "bypassed the permit system"
- "conducted non-routine work without approval"

================================================================================

RULE 7: SAFE DRIVING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Description:
Vehicle-related incidents including speeding, distracted driving, lack of 
seatbelt, poor road conditions, driver fatigue, or lack of defensive driving.

Keywords:
driving, vehicle, truck, transport, crash, rollover, speeding, distracted, 
road condition, mud, rain, fog, driver fatigue, seatbelt, collision, head-on

Example Phrases:
- "lost control on a muddy road"
- "driver fell asleep at the wheel"
- "speeding on a curve"
- "no seatbelt worn"
- "distracted driver caused collision"
- "vehicle rolled over on the highway"

================================================================================

RULE 8: WORKING AT HEIGHT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Description:
Falls from height due to lack of fall protection, improper use of harnesses, 
defective scaffolding, ladders, or guardrails.

Keywords:
height, scaffolding, ladder, harness, fall protection, guardrail, elevated work, 
climbing, slip, work platform, fall arrest, safety net

Example Phrases:
- "fell from a ladder"
- "no harness worn"
- "scaffolding collapsed"
- "working without guardrails"
- "climbed without fall protection"
- "fell 10 feet from an elevated platform"

================================================================================

RULE 9: MANAGEMENT OF CHANGE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Description:
Unsafe or unapproved changes to procedures, equipment, personnel, or operating 
conditions without a proper risk assessment, revalidation, or authorization.

Keywords:
change, procedure, modification, deviation, new equipment, unapproved change, 
risk assessment, revalidation, temporary change, personnel change, MOC

Example Phrases:
- "changed the procedure without review"
- "modified equipment without authorization"
- "unapproved deviation from standard"
- "new process without risk assessment"
- "bypassed management of change"
- "temporary change became permanent"
